Meet Drew

About Drew

Cosmetology has always been my passion. I knew from an early age that I wanted a career that combined creativity, connection, and helping people feel confident. After graduating high school, I jumped straight into cosmetology school and never looked back.

Today, with more than 7 years of experience behind the chair, I continue to love every part of this industry. Creating customized color, helping clients feel beautiful, and building lasting relationships are some of my favorite parts of what I do.

Hi, I'm Drew!

I specialize in balayage — the technique behind everything from dimensional blondes to low-maintenance highlights — along with modern blonding techniques. I also love creating vibrant pops of color for guests looking for something bold and unique.

While I work with all hair types and textures, my passion lies in creating beautiful color that complements your lifestyle and grows out gracefully. I believe great hair should look amazing both on the day you leave the salon and for weeks afterward.

Continuing education is incredibly important to me. I stay up to date on current trends, advanced color techniques, and the latest products to ensure every guest receives modern, high-quality results.

My goal is to make every appointment feel luxurious, relaxing, and fun. I want you to leave feeling confident, refreshed, and excited about your hair.

I can't wait to meet you and help bring your hair goals to life.

Drew
